## 1. Study Objective and Research Question
In oncology surveillance, risk models and biomarker checks are evaluated repeatedly over multi-year follow-up (up to 20 serial visits). Under standard per-visit thresholding, the cumulative patient-level false alarm rate can inflate across looks. Conversely, sequential e-processes provide mathematically guaranteed anytime validity under unlimited looks, but may pay an empirical power cost.

**Core Research Questions:**
1. Does Model K2 (`vigil_ml.k2.SequentialDetector`), with its within-patient log-scale noise standard deviation $\sigma_{\log}$ estimated from a disjoint calibration cohort, strictly bound the cumulative patient-level false-alarm rate across all stress scenarios?
2. When compared **at a matched patient-level false-alarm rate** on the project simulator (`vigil-sim`), what is the empirical power difference and detection delay between K2's anytime-valid e-process and the static threshold rule?

## 2. Pre-Registered Hypotheses & Programmatic Verdict Criteria

- **H1 (Matched-FAR Sensitivity Comparison):**
  - Evaluated on `vigil-sim` (test seed 42, $N=500$ patients) by sweeping the K2 operating parameter $\alpha_{\text{pt}}$ and the static threshold cutoff until their null patient-level false alarm rates match a common target operating point ($\text{FAR}_{\text{pt}} \approx 1\%$).
  - Criterion: Calculate paired difference in recurrence sensitivity $\Delta \text{Power} = \text{Power}_{\text{K2}} - \text{Power}_{\text{Static}}$ with a 1,000-sample patient bootstrap 95% confidence interval.
  - Programmatic Verdict:
    - If 95% CI lower bound $> 0$: `H1 CONFIRMED: K2 achieves superior sensitivity at matched false-alarm rate.`
    - If 95% CI upper bound $< 0$: `H1 REFUTED: K2 pays an empirical power cost (lower sensitivity) for its anytime-valid guarantee at matched false-alarm rate.`
    - If 95% CI contains 0: `H1 INCONCLUSIVE: K2 and static rule exhibit statistically indistinguishable sensitivity at matched false-alarm rate.`
  - The verdict string must be generated by code directly from the bootstrap CI (RR7, RR14).

- **H2 (Supermartingale Validity Guarantee):**
  - Criterion: Empirical patient-level false alarm rate $\le \alpha_{\text{pt}} + 0.02$ across all 6 scenarios in the acceptance stress suite (Gaussian $\sigma=0.10$, Gaussian $\sigma=0.12$, heavy-tailed Student-$t_5$, AR(1) $\rho=0.3$, long-horizon 60 visits, and 30% missingness).
  - Programmatic Verdict: `CONFIRMED` if $\max(\text{FAR}) \le 0.0700$, else `VIOLATED`.

- **H3 (Power and Detection Delay under True Doubling):**
  - Criterion: On progressive recurrences with known tumor doubling times, K2 achieves $\ge 75\%$ power with median delay $\le 240$ days on moderate progression ($T_d = 90$d), and $\ge 85\%$ power on rapid progression ($T_d = 60$d).
  - Programmatic Verdict: `CONFIRMED` if both conditions are satisfied, else `NOT MET`.

## 3. Disjoint Calibration Cohort Protocol (RR11)
To prevent tuning on evaluation cohorts:
- Within-patient log-scale marker variation $\sigma_{\log}$ is estimated strictly on an independent calibration cohort (`vigil-sim` G1_SMOOTH, seed 999, $N=500$ null patients) disjoint from test seed 42.
- The estimated $\hat{\sigma}_{\log}$ is computed as the root mean square of within-patient log differences across consecutive visits:
  $$\hat{\sigma}_{\log} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2 M} \sum_{i} \sum_{t} (\ln y_{i,t} - \ln y_{i,t-1})^2}$$
- This fitted $\hat{\sigma}_{\log}$ is supplied to `SequentialDetector(sigma_log=...)`.

## 4. Primary and Secondary Endpoints
1. **Primary Endpoints:**
   - Null patient-level false alarm rate ($\text{FAR}_{\text{pt}}$).
   - Recurrence detection power at matched $\text{FAR}_{\text{pt}}$.
   - Detection delay (days from onset to alert) among detected cases.
2. **Statistical Uncertainty:**
   - 1,000-sample bootstrap 95% confidence intervals over patients for all reported metrics (RR14).
