# Proposal: define K1 event-time semantics before accepting recurrence risk

## Finding

The immutable K1 contract requires `risk_from_posterior(samples, horizon_days)` to be a recurrence probability derived from the four parameter samples, and requires that value to increase when `doubling_time_days` is shortened. The declared parameters describe `onset_day` as the recurrence **seeding** day, `doubling_time_days` as growth after seeding, and CA 15-3 shedding/clearance. They do not define a clinical recurrence observation/detection event, baseline state at a landmark, threshold for that event, or a mapping from latent burden to the simulator's recurrence label.

In `vigil-sim`, recurrence presence and time are generated from stage/subtype probabilities and gamma-distributed recurrence times (see `packages/vigil-sim/vigil_sim/generators.py::sample_baseline`). Its CA 15-3 trajectory is generated by a separate marker process with patient baseline, assay/laboratory effects, treatment, visit, missingness and verification behavior. K1's four-parameter toy dynamics have none of those links. A function of the four parameters that is monotone in doubling time therefore cannot, without an added event definition and model, be interpreted as the simulator's recurrence-horizon probability. The current logistic burden transform and fixed threshold in `vigil_ml.k1.risk_from_posterior` do not supply that missing event model.

## Proposed disposition

1. Keep the immutable contract and acceptance tests unchanged pending overseer decision.
2. Until the estimand is clarified, retain `risk_from_posterior` only as an explicitly synthetic research diagnostic or disable recurrence-risk output in `HybridKineticModelK1`; do not label it a probability, calibrate it against outcomes, or expose it in product runtime.
3. For recurrence prediction, specify a separate event-time generative link from latent kinetic state to the simulator's true event time, competing events, censoring and observation process. Register the event definition and a new experiment before fitting or frozen-cohort access.
4. If the intended K1 target is instead posterior probability of a latent burden/marker threshold crossing, name and define that event, source or label its threshold as a synthetic assumption, and do not score it against recurrence labels.

## Evidence and boundary

This proposal compares `docs/ml/K1-CONTRACT.md`, `packages/vigil-ml/vigil_ml/k1.py::risk_from_posterior`, and `packages/vigil-sim/vigil_sim/generators.py::sample_baseline`. It does not change the overseer-owned test, claim a performance result, or determine the correct clinical estimand. Engineering work not dependent on this decision continues under EXP-K1-004.

## Decision needed

Overseer review should provide the intended K1 event definition and whether the existing monotonicity requirement is a software invariant or a recurrence-risk scientific requirement. Until that decision is recorded, recurrence-risk validity is NOT MET and K1 stays research-only.
