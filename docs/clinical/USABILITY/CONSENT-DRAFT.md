# Usability session consent — draft

This form is a draft for ethics and site review. It is not an approved consent form and does not authorise recruitment.

## Study activity

You are invited to try a synthetic-data software prototype. The session evaluates interface clarity and task flow. It is not clinical care, clinical validation, or a test of your professional ability.

## Voluntary participation

Taking part is your choice. You may skip any task, pause, or leave at any time without penalty or effect on your work or care.

## Information collected

If approved, the study team may record task completion, comments, and usability observations. Audio or video recording is optional and requires separate consent. Do not provide patient names, identifiers, records, or other clinical information. The team will explain the approved storage, access, retention, and deletion plan before recruitment begins.

## Risks and limits

The prototype can be wrong or confusing. It must not be used to make patient-care decisions. Report any interpretation that sounds diagnostic, falsely reassuring, or more certain than the evidence supports.

## Questions and choice

Before this draft is used, the approved protocol must name the responsible investigator, contact details, ethics reference, data controller, retention period, and complaint route. The participant receives a copy of the final approved form.

**Draft acknowledgement:** no signature should be collected against this draft.
