# Clinician workstation usability moderator script — draft

**Use status:** draft for ethics and site review. No participant recruitment or recording is authorised by this document.

## Before the session

- Use the synthetic demo environment only. Do not enter real patient identifiers or clinical data.
- Confirm local ethics, facility, and data-protection approvals before scheduling any participant.
- Obtain the participant's consent before recording. The participant may stop at any time without explanation.
- Ask about assistive technology and preferred language; do not treat machine-drafted translations as reviewed.

## Opening statement

“We are evaluating this prototype, not you. It is a research decision-support system and is not validated for patient care. Please think aloud as you use it. You may skip any task or stop. The system must not be used to diagnose, reassure, or order tests.”

## Tasks

1. Find the M4 test ladder and explain what the evidence ranges do and do not say.
2. Identify whether the facility data are illustrative, stale, or unverified.
3. Explain the warning shown after a negative result and identify who decides the next step.
4. Submit a synthetic availability/cost report and locate the audit confirmation.
5. Open a companion explanation, check its disclosure state, and try read-aloud. Report what happens if audio is unavailable.

## Neutral probes

- “What do you think this screen is telling you?”
- “What would you do next, and who would make that decision?”
- “Which part was unclear or could lead to a wrong conclusion?”
- “Did anything sound more certain than the evidence supports?”

Do not teach the interface during the task. Record observed task outcome, hesitations, misinterpretations, and participant wording separately. Do not convert a small convenience sample into a usability or safety claim.

## Stop conditions

Stop the task if a participant interprets an output as a diagnosis, a negative test as proof of absence, or the interface as an order. Record the event without patient information and refer it to the safety lead before any further sessions.
