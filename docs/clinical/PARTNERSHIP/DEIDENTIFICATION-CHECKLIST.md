# De-identification checklist (data steward, before any extract leaves the hospital)

> **Status: DRAFT-UNREVIEWED.** A working checklist written by the engineering team. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by the hospital's data-protection officer or by counsel. The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the hospital's own policy decide what is permitted; where this list is weaker, the stricter rule applies (see `DPIA.md`).

The extraction format (`DATA-DICTIONARY.md`) is built so that most identifiers cannot be expressed in it. The checklist covers what the format cannot enforce. Tick each item; the signed copy is kept by the hospital, not sent to Vigil.

## A. Before extraction
- [ ] Ethics approval (or waiver) and the data-use agreement are in force; the extraction is within their scope (`ETHICS-APPLICATION.md`, `DUA-TEMPLATE.md`).
- [ ] The data steward, not an engineer, runs the extraction and holds the re-identification key.
- [ ] Pseudonyms are generated by the steward. Either a random mapping table kept at the hospital, or `contracts.partner.hl7_onsite.hmac_pseudonym` with a secret of at least 16 bytes that stays at the hospital. The pseudonym is never derived from the hospital number, name, phone, date of birth or any other identifier without a secret.
- [ ] Dates: every date is converted to a **relative day** from diagnosis. The map from patient to diagnosis date stays at the hospital.

## B. In the files
- [ ] No names, initials, addresses, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, hospital or folder numbers, national ID numbers, next of kin, photographs or scans.
- [ ] No free-text columns. (The ingestion refuses unknown columns precisely because free text is where names hide.)
- [ ] No absolute dates anywhere, including Excel date cells and dates inside text.
- [ ] Age is in years at diagnosis, not a date of birth.
- [ ] No facility-level or geographic detail finer than the facility itself; the extraction is a single site unless the protocol says otherwise.
- [ ] Rare combinations (for example a very young patient with a rare histology in a small cohort) are reviewed by the steward for re-identification risk; the suppression threshold used in reports is PR-06 of the pre-registration, and the steward may require a higher one.

## C. Before sending
- [ ] Run `scripts/run_partner_ingest.py <folder or workbook>` **inside the hospital**, on the hospital's own machine. A refusal (`IDENTIFIER_COLUMN`, `UNKNOWN_COLUMN`, `IDENTIFIER_VALUE`) must be fixed at the source, not by renaming a column.
- [ ] Read the rejection report with the steward. A rejection rate above the stop rate (PR-07) means the extraction mapping is wrong; fix it, do not send.
- [ ] Implausible laboratory values listed in the report are checked against the chart by the steward and corrected at the source or confirmed.
- [ ] The files and the report are transferred by the channel agreed in the data-use agreement; the pseudonym secret, the date map and the re-identification key are not part of the transfer.
- [ ] The steward signs and dates this checklist and files it with the agreement.

## D. What Vigil does on receipt
- Re-runs the same ingestion; a refusal is returned to the steward and nothing is parsed.
- Never attempts re-identification, never links with other datasets unless the agreement allows it, and reports small cells only above the suppression threshold.
