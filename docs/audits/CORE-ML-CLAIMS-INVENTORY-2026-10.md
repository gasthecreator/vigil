2. `reports/ml/BENCHMARK_REPORT.md` and `docs/models/MODEL-CARD-K3.md` now have explicit retraction banners; the aggregate runner fails closed, and S3 has a sidecar retraction with historical outputs unchanged. `reports/ml/results.json`, S1/S9 reports/cards, and remaining K1/K2/K3 claims still require generated-output/source reconciliation (package G).
3. Q-7 B fixed simulator export defects on this branch: `get_unleaked_training_examples` leaked unmeasured simulator values and treated early-censored outcomes as negative; oracle labels could include latent recurrences after loss to follow-up; visits could continue after confirmed recurrence; historical profiles lacked shared facility shifts and imaging-result/specificity/propensity records. The separately versioned K3 v2 profile now declares facility, verification, and assay-missingness assumptions. Positive screening results below configured visibility do not become confirmed diagnosis labels; surveillance stops 90 days after confirmation. Tests cover these contracts. A deeper mechanism audit found current G1 does not implement the documented ODE/clearance model and G2 does not implement Poisson shot noise; source and dataset-card descriptions now reflect actual behavior, but a corrected simulator and preregistered reruns remain required. EXP-CORE-ML-005 is immutable process evidence for source `ce897a1`; 4/30 propensity decile estimates were just outside unadjusted Wilson intervals. EXP-CORE-ML-006 independently audited the corrected source: zero post-diagnosis-window or post-competing-event observations among 30,000 synthetic patients. Verification-bias estimators and the observed-diagnosis versus latent-recurrence estimand remain open. Full audit of every historical training/evaluation and serving path is still open.
4. K2, K3, K8 ensemble, M1, M3/M5, M12, registry and production/runtime consumers still need the per-source, data, call-site, test, and claim audit required by packages E/F. This inventory does not imply those reviews are complete.

6. ADR-0002's quarantine decision was not enforced at the source entry points: the legacy baseline and LSTM trainers remained runnable and could recreate invalid models.
