# Core ML repair and evidence-closure plan — 2026-10

## Purpose and boundary

This plan closes the model-engine defects and unsupported claims recorded in [`CORE-ML-RERUN-2026-10.md`](CORE-ML-RERUN-2026-10.md), traces them to the Master Plan, ML Program, phase prompts and committed evidence, and makes every fix reproducible. It covers the ML engine end to end: data generation and labels, feature construction, fitting, calibration, uncertainty, inference, model selection, runtime/export, integration, user-facing claims, and the studies/model cards that support them.

“Optimal” means the best model under a preregistered, safety-relevant estimand and measured constraints. No clinical optimum or real-population performance can be established from synthetic data. Real-data validation, external reproduction, and prospective validation remain blocked until the owner obtains the data, approvals, partner and independent reviewers identified in the Master Plan; all preparation that does not need those inputs remains in scope.

## Governing rules

- Follow `AGENTS.md`, `MASTER_PLAN.md` (immutable), `ML_PROGRAM.md`, `EVIDENCE.md`, the current progress ledger, and P0–P16 prompts. If a governing acceptance test or plan item appears wrong, add evidence to `docs/proposals/`; do not edit overseer-owned tests, verdicts, maturity, or ledger rows.
- Preserve invalid historical artifacts and their retractions; never overwrite, hand-edit, or silently rehabilitate a result. All new numerical results come from committed scripts after a committed preregistration and clean implementation SHA, with frozen test access logged once.
- Keep predictions, risk statements, posterior intervals, and explanations unavailable or explicitly research-only until their estimand, calibration, uncertainty, safety, and runtime contracts pass. No code-literal clinical threshold or assumption without a versioned, sourced configuration and sensitivity analysis.
- Every model comparison uses patient-level splits and resampling, censoring/competing-risk-aware estimands, calibration and decision-relevant metrics, multiple-seed and robustness analysis, leak canaries, negative results, and an explicit keep/fallback/eliminate decision. Use the ML Program’s fallback/elimination protocol; do not stop after one failed configuration.
- Work on the existing `codex/quality-rerun-core` branch and PR #79; never merge while required gates or scientific validity conditions are open. Keep changes reviewable and update the report/ledger with evidence as work closes.

## Execution order

1. A–B: freeze the inventory and close data/estimand integrity before any additional tuning.
2. C: get the old score floors out of the way through the documented proposal process; preserve failures in the meantime.
3. D–E: repair K1 and K3, the two invalid core-model identities, each with preregistration before test access.
4. F–G: audit every remaining model/evidence artifact against the corrected contract; repair or regenerate each affected item, preserving negative results.
5. H–I: reproduce the complete synthetic evidence set from a clean checkout, finish software gates and safety artifacts, and prepare—but do not overclaim—human-dependent validation.

Packages A–I are required for closure. They may be split into reviewable commits/PRs in this branch, but none is silently dropped. The phase stops for the governing review verdict only after its report, ledger, tests, and artifacts are committed. No result-producing code or frozen test access precedes its preregistration.
