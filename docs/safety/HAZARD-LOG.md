# Vigil — Hazard Log (FMEA-Style)

> **Document Status:** Active. Version 0.1 (Phase 0 Baseline).  
> **Standard:** Aligned with ISO 14971 (Medical devices — Application of risk management to medical devices) and IEC 62304.  
> **Rule:** Every hazard that could result in patient harm or compromised decision-making must have defined mitigations with verification evidence.

## Hazard Log v0

| ID | Hazard | Potential Clinical Harm | Severity | Probability | Mitigations (Design & Architectural Controls) | Verification Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | False reassurance (low risk score read as "fine") | Delayed presentation; missed recurrence — the exact original harm | Critical | Moderate | • Never show "low risk = safe"; UI displays "no signal detected in the data available".<br>• Explicit abstention states (`insufficient_data`).<br>• Regular follow-up reminders continue regardless of score.<br>• Sensitivity reported with confidence intervals.<br>• Surveillance intervals are never auto-lengthened. | Phase 0 UI disclaimer; Phase 3 abstention tests |
| **H2** | False alarm | Patient panic, unnecessary expense, invasive follow-up imaging | Moderate | Moderate | • Calibrated alert tiers (`watch`, `review`, `urgent`) with conformal $\alpha$-budgets.<br>• Mandatory confirm-by-repeat pathway (M3 Plausibility Adjudicator).<br>• Family UI displays calibrated uncertainty bands rather than raw alarmist probabilities.<br>• Continuous alert-burden monitoring in CI. | `scripts/check_claims.py`; M3 test suite |
| **H3** | Wrong-patient or mis-entered data | Erroneous risk trajectory and misdirected clinical triage | Severe | Low | • Two-identifier cross-checks on ingestion.<br>• Duplicate detection engine.<br>• Versioned records with immutable audit logs.<br>• Correction workflows preserving historical audit trail. | M0 data model tests |
| **H4** | Unit, decimal, or analyte mix-up | Large spurious biomarker shift triggering unwarranted escalation | Severe | Moderate | • Strict UCUM unit parsing and LOINC validation in `vigil-core`.<br>• Strict physiological plausibility bounds.<br>• Delta-check validation.<br>• Explicit confirmation modal for out-of-bounds readings; zero silent unit coercion. | `vigil-core` property tests (WP1.1) |
| **H5** | Model drift / dataset shift | Undetected performance decay over time | Severe | Moderate | • Continuous data-drift monitoring (PSI/KS tests).<br>• Conformal coverage tracking.<br>• Shadow deployment before model promotion.<br>• Automated rollback triggers. | M11 monitoring harness (Phase 3) |
| **H6** | Health data breach | Privacy violation, patient stigmatization | Critical | Low | • Field-level encryption for direct identifiers.<br>• Zero wildcard CORS (`*`).<br>• Role-based access control (RBAC).<br>• Tamper-evident hash-chained audit logging.<br>• Air-gapped on-premise deployment option. | ADR-0004; Security audit |
| **H7** | Automation bias | Clinician unthinkingly defers to automated score | Severe | Moderate | • Display provenance, inputs, and uncertainty intervals.<br>• Mandatory disposition and clinician rationale for alert dismissals.<br>• Clinician override is always respected and preserved in the audit trail. | M5 narrative engine (Phase 4) |
| **H8** | Inequitable subgroup performance | Substandard surveillance for specific demographic or socioeconomic cohorts | Critical | Moderate | • Mandatory subgroup evaluation gates across age, stage, subtype, and data completeness.<br>• Release blocked if subgroup performance falls below max-gap rule (MASTER_PLAN §8.3). | M11 evaluation suite |
| **H9** | Privacy leak on shared mobile device | Unwanted disclosure of oncology diagnosis | Severe | Moderate | • Outbound notifications (SMS/WhatsApp) use generic non-diagnostic template text.<br>• PIN-gated access to sensitive clinical details.<br>• Per-patient communication preference registry. | M6 Follow-Up Guardian (Phase 5) |
| **H10** | Radiotherapy queue triage inequity or failure | Unfair treatment delay exacerbating recurrence risk | Critical | Low | • Bounded waiting-time disparity fairness constraints.<br>• Indication-specific harm curves (E8).<br>• Transparent justification for every queue reordering.<br>• Clinician override recorded and respected. | M7 CP-SAT optimizer (Phase 7) |
| **H11** | LLM narrative hallucination | Misleading or fabricated clinical statements | Critical | Moderate | • Hard fact-table verifier rejects any text referencing non-computed facts or forbidden claims.<br>• Deterministic template fallback when LLM is unverified or disabled.<br>• LLM disabled by default on clinical alert critical paths. | M5 fact-table verifier (Phase 4) |
| **H12** | Stale diagnostic capability data | Patient referred for unavailable or defunct imaging modality | Moderate | Moderate | • `verified_on` timestamp per facility capability row.<br>• Stale rows (>12 months) down-weighted and prominently flagged.<br>• Direct clinical referral feedback loops. | M4 Test Ladder (Phase 4) |
| **H13** | System outage during clinic hours | Interrupted patient care in high-volume clinics | Severe | Moderate | • Offline-first client architecture with local storage sync.<br>• Printable clinic dossiers and paper backup exports.<br>• Documented SLOs and operational runbooks. | PWA offline tests (Phase 6) |
| **H14** | Family misinterprets numeric probability | Unnecessary emotional distress or dangerous complacency | Severe | High | • Family companion hides raw decimal percentages by default.<br>• Uses calibrated qualitative bands and structured "Questions for your Doctor".<br>• Dedicated "Bad-News Mode" allowing controlled delivery. | M8 companion specifications (Phase 6) |
| **H15** | Synthetic training data mistaken for real clinical data | Erroneous clinical adoption based on ungrounded results | Critical | High | • Mandatory `synthetic=true` metadata tag on all mock rows.<br>• Persistent UI badge and API response flags.<br>• Automated claims-policy CI enforcement (`scripts/check_claims.py`). | `scripts/check_claims.py` |
| **H16** | Over-reliance on evidence-limited radiotherapy harm curves | Suboptimal scheduling decisions | Moderate | Moderate | • Transparent evidence badges and uncertainty intervals attached to harm parameters.<br>• Sensitivity analysis across multiple parameter regimes.<br>• Never auto-applied without human scheduler sign-off. | M7 simulation reports (Phase 7) |
