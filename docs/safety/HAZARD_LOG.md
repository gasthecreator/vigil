# Vigil — Hazard Log (FMEA-Style)

> **Document Status:** Active. Version 1.0 (Phase 8a Baseline).  
> **Standard:** Aligned with ISO 14971:2019 (Medical devices — Application of risk management to medical devices) and IEC 62304:2006/AMD 1:2015.  
> **Rule:** Every hazard that could result in patient harm, clinician misdirection, or security breach must have defined mitigations with verifiable automated test links.

## Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)

| ID | Hazard | Potential Clinical Harm | Severity | Probability | Mitigations (Design & Architectural Controls) | Verification Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | False reassurance (low risk score read as "fine") | Delayed presentation; missed recurrence — the original clinical harm | Critical | Moderate | • Never show "low risk = safe"; UI displays "no signal detected in data available".<br>• Explicit abstention states (`insufficient_data`).<br>• Regular follow-up reminders continue regardless of score.<br>• Sensitivity reported with confidence intervals.<br>• Surveillance intervals are never auto-lengthened. | `tests/acceptance_p4/test_m5_contract.py::test_forbidden_claims_rejected` |
| **H2** | False alarm | Patient panic, unnecessary expense, invasive follow-up imaging | Moderate | Moderate | • Calibrated alert tiers (`watch`, `review`, `urgent`) with conformal $\alpha$-budgets.<br>• Mandatory confirm-by-repeat pathway (M3 Plausibility Adjudicator).<br>• Family UI displays calibrated uncertainty bands rather than raw alarmist probabilities.<br>• Continuous alert-burden monitoring in CI. | `tests/acceptance_p4/test_m3_contract.py::test_calibration` |
| **H3** | Wrong-patient or mis-entered data | Erroneous risk trajectory and misdirected clinical triage | Severe | Low | • Two-identifier cross-checks on ingestion.<br>• Duplicate detection engine.<br>• Versioned records with immutable audit logs.<br>• Correction workflows preserving historical audit trail. | `tests/acceptance_p7/test_p7_contract.py::test_feature_matrix_is_invariant_to_latent_columns` |
| **H4** | Unit, decimal, or analyte mix-up | Large spurious biomarker shift triggering unwarranted escalation | Severe | Moderate | • Strict UCUM unit parsing and LOINC validation in `vigil-core`.<br>• Strict physiological plausibility bounds.<br>• Delta-check validation.<br>• Explicit confirmation modal for out-of-bounds readings; zero silent unit coercion. | `packages/vigil-core/tests/test_units.py::test_incompatible_conversions_raise_explicit_error` |
| **H5** | Model drift / dataset shift | Undetected performance decay over time | Severe | Moderate | • Continuous data-drift monitoring (PSI/KS tests).<br>• Conformal coverage tracking.<br>• Shadow deployment before model promotion.<br>• Automated rollback triggers. | `tests/acceptance_3b/test_k8_contract.py::test_adaptive_conformal_tracks_drift` |
| **H6** | Health data breach | Privacy violation, patient stigmatization | Critical | Low | • Field-level encryption for direct identifiers.<br>• Zero wildcard CORS (`*`).<br>• Role-based access control (RBAC).<br>• Tamper-evident hash-chained audit logging.<br>• Air-gapped on-premise deployment option. | `tests/acceptance_p5/test_api_security.py::test_reads_require_consent_for_every_role` |
| **H7** | Automation bias | Clinician unthinkingly defers to automated score | Severe | Moderate | • Display provenance, inputs, and uncertainty intervals.<br>• Mandatory disposition and clinician rationale for alert dismissals.<br>• Clinician override is always respected and preserved in the audit trail. | `tests/acceptance_p4/test_m5_contract_v2.py::test_every_sentence_must_be_derivable` |
| **H8** | Inequitable subgroup performance | Substandard surveillance for specific demographic or socioeconomic cohorts | Critical | Moderate | • Mandatory subgroup evaluation gates across age, stage, subtype, and data completeness.<br>• Release blocked if subgroup performance falls below max-gap rule (MASTER_PLAN §8.3). | `packages/vigil-ml/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_subgroup_fairness_disparity_bound` |
| **H9** | Privacy leak on shared mobile device | Unwanted disclosure of oncology diagnosis | Severe | Moderate | • Outbound notifications (SMS/WhatsApp) use generic non-diagnostic template text.<br>• PIN-gated access to sensitive clinical details.<br>• Per-patient communication preference registry. | `tests/acceptance_p9/test_p9_contract.py::test_identifiers_are_not_returned_without_consent` |
| **H10** | Radiotherapy queue triage inequity or failure | Unfair treatment delay exacerbating recurrence risk | Critical | Low | • Bounded waiting-time disparity fairness constraints.<br>• Indication-specific harm curves (E8).<br>• Transparent justification for every queue reordering.<br>• Clinician override recorded and respected. | `packages/vigil-ml/tests/test_scheduler.py::test_no_starvation_w_max_invariant` |
| **H11** | LLM narrative hallucination | Misleading or fabricated clinical statements | Critical | Moderate | • Hard fact-table verifier rejects any text referencing non-computed facts or forbidden claims.<br>• Deterministic template fallback when LLM is unverified or disabled.<br>• LLM disabled by default on clinical alert critical paths. | `tests/acceptance_p4/test_m5_contract.py::test_number_mutations_rejected` |
| **H12** | Stale diagnostic capability data | Patient referred for unavailable or defunct imaging modality | Moderate | Moderate | • `verified_on` timestamp per facility capability row.<br>• Stale rows (>12 months) down-weighted and prominently flagged.<br>• Direct clinical referral feedback loops. | `packages/contracts/tests/test_contract_drift.py::test_openapi_spec_has_no_drift` |
| **H13** | System outage during clinic hours | Interrupted patient care in high-volume clinics | Severe | Moderate | • Offline-first client architecture with local storage sync.<br>• Printable clinic dossiers and paper backup exports.<br>• Documented SLOs and operational runbooks. | `tests/acceptance_p8/test_p8_contract.py::test_sync_replay_does_not_bypass_consent_or_leak_other_patients` |
| **H14** | Family misinterprets numeric probability | Unnecessary emotional distress or dangerous complacency | Severe | High | • Family companion hides raw decimal percentages by default.<br>• Uses calibrated qualitative bands and structured "Questions for your Doctor".<br>• Dedicated "Bad-News Mode" allowing controlled delivery. | `packages/vigil-ml/tests/test_m5_plain_language.py::test_plain_language_explainer_reading_level_and_length` |
| **H15** | Synthetic training data mistaken for real clinical data | Erroneous clinical adoption based on ungrounded results | Critical | High | • Mandatory `synthetic=true` metadata tag on all mock rows.<br>• Persistent UI badge and API response flags.<br>• Automated claims-policy CI enforcement (`scripts/check_claims.py`). | `tests/acceptance_p13/test_p13_contract.py::test_quality_audit_never_mixes_synthetic_trajectories_into_facility_data` |
| **H16** | Over-reliance on evidence-limited radiotherapy harm curves | Suboptimal scheduling decisions | Moderate | Moderate | • Transparent evidence badges and uncertainty intervals attached to harm parameters.<br>• Sensitivity analysis across multiple parameter regimes.<br>• Never auto-applied without human scheduler sign-off. | `packages/vigil-ml/tests/test_continuity_harm.py::test_supported_contexts_and_evidence_citations` |
| **H17** | Premature disclosure of bad news / escalate results | Patient receives unmediated relapse finding before clinician consultation | Critical | Moderate | • Server-side bad-news gate (`disclosure_unlocked_at` on Patient).<br>• Clinician/Admin only `POST /disclosure/unlock`.<br>• Plain language explainer returns 403 `disclosure_locked` for escalate results until unlocked. | `tests/acceptance_p11/test_p11_contract.py::test_bad_news_is_withheld_by_the_server_until_a_clinician_releases_it` |
| **H18** | Companion / Caregiver bearer token compromise | Unauthorized third party gains access to patient clinical dossier | Critical | Low | • Companion bearer tokens hashed at rest using SHA-256.<br>• Returned exactly once at issuance; never listed or re-exposed in plain text.<br>• Server enforces 30-day expiration and explicit clinician revocation. | `tests/acceptance_p8/test_p8_contract.py::test_mrn_sent_through_the_api_is_stored_encrypted` |
| **H19** | Quality audit engine masks missed recurrences | Clinician misses recurrence signals due to automated task creation | Severe | Moderate | • `action_recorded` attribution restricted strictly to explicit clinician review/acknowledgement.<br>• System-generated background tasks explicitly excluded from action attribution.<br>• Pipeline-only validation test verifies non-zero missed rates. | `packages/vigil-ml/tests/test_quality.py::test_pipeline_only_facility_produces_non_zero_missed_rate` |
| **H20** | Tampering with delay ledger or clinical audit records | Malicious or accidental alteration of historical clinical tracking | Critical | Low | • Hash-chained tamper-evident append-only ledger.<br>• Server-side derivation of all ledger delay and harm metrics (`/schedule/confirm`).<br>• Audit tail verification detects deletions even if entire database is rewritten. | `tests/acceptance_p7/test_p7_contract.py::test_audit_tail_deletion_is_detected_even_if_the_whole_database_is_rewritten` |
| **H21** | Processing data after patient consent withdrawal | Regulatory violation and ethical breach under GDPR/NDPR | Severe | Moderate | • Strict server-side consent verification on all clinical routes.<br>• HTTP-level revocation test verifies immediate 403 block upon consent withdrawal.<br>• Re-grant workflow restores clinical access. | `tests/acceptance_p12/test_p12_contract.py::test_consent_withdrawal_over_http_revokes_access_and_regrant_restores_it` |
| **H22** | Supply chain compromise or insecure default secrets | Full application compromise via default credentials or hijacked package | Critical | Low | • Production startup rejects default or hardcoded JWT secrets.<br>• Automated Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) generation (CycloneDX).<br>• Pinned dependencies and automated vulnerability scanning in CI. | `tests/acceptance_p5/test_api_security.py::test_default_jwt_secret_is_not_accepted_in_production` |
