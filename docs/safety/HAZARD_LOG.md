# Vigil — Hazard Log (FMEA-Style)

> **Document Status:** Active. Version 1.1 (Phase 8b Release Readiness).  
> **Standard:** Aligned with ISO 14971:2019 (Medical devices — Application of risk management to medical devices) and IEC 62304:2006/AMD 1:2015.  
> **Rule:** Every hazard that could result in patient harm, clinician misdirection, or security breach must have defined mitigations with verifiable automated test links. Any hazard whose control is not fully proven in simulation or real-world evidence is classified as **OPEN** with residual risk stated plainly.
