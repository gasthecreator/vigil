## 2. Verbatim Statutory Provisions & Articles

### Section 30 — Sensitive Personal Data
> **Section 30(1):**
> "A data controller or data processor shall not process sensitive personal data, unless:
> (a) the data subject has given consent to the processing for one or more specified purposes;
> (b) the processing is necessary for the purposes of performing obligations and exercising specific rights of the data controller or of the data subject in the field of employment and social security and social protection law...;
> (c) the processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or of another individual...;
> (d) the processing is carried out in the course of its legitimate activities with appropriate safeguards by a foundation, association or any other not-for-profit body with a political, philosophical, religious or trade union aim...;
> (e) the processing relates to personal data which are manifestly made public by the data subject;
> (f) the processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims;
> (g) the processing is necessary for reasons of substantial public interest;
> (h) the processing is necessary for the purposes of preventive or occupational medicine, for the assessment of the working capacity of the employee, medical diagnosis, the provision of health or social care or treatment or the management of health or social care systems and services...; or
> (i) the processing is necessary for reasons of public interest in the area of public health... or archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes."

### Section 28 — Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
> **Section 28(1):**
> "Where the processing of personal data may result in high risk to the rights and freedoms of a data subject by virtue of its nature, scope, context and purposes, a data controller shall, prior to the processing, carry out a data protection impact assessment."
> 
> **Section 28(2):**
> "A data protection impact assessment under subsection (1) shall contain:
> (a) a systematic description of the envisaged processing operations and the purposes of the processing...;
> (b) an assessment of the necessity and proportionality of the processing operations...;
> (c) an assessment of the risks to the rights and freedoms of data subjects...; and
> (d) the measures envisaged to address the risks, including safeguards, security measures and mechanisms to ensure the protection of personal data."

### Section 41–43 — Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Data
> Requires adequate data protection safeguards or binding agreements when sensitive health data is processed outside Nigeria, or processing within Nigeria using local infrastructure.

## 3. How These Provisions Are Enforced in Vigil

1. **Partner Hospital Data Protection Impact Assessment (`docs/clinical/PARTNERSHIP/DPIA.md`):**
   - Directly executes the statutory requirements of Section 28.
   - Evaluates risk vectors, minimization, on-site pseudonymization, and cryptographic protection.
2. **Lawful Gateways under Section 30(1)(h) & (i):**
   - Provides the legal basis for secondary research on de-identified retrospective oncology records under Institutional Health Research Ethics Committee (HREC) approval and waiver of consent.
3. **Data Localization and Security Controls:**
   - Complies with cross-border rules by enforcing local execution and zero transmission of unencrypted identifiable patient records to external cloud endpoints.
