## Verbatim Excerpt: Results (Site of Distant Metastases)

From Results ("Site of distant metastases"):
> "The study groups consisted of 243,896 patients, including 226,451 cases in control groups and 17,445 cases with distant metastasis. Bone metastasis was found in 8848 cases, compared with 1,000 brain metastasis cases, 3434 liver metastasis cases and 4167 lung metastasis cases."
