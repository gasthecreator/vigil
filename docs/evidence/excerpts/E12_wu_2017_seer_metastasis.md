## Verbatim Excerpt: Table 1 & Results (Distribution of Distant Metastases)

From Results ("Site of distant metastases"):
> "Among the 17,449 patients with documented sites of distant metastases:
> - Bone metastases: 8,848 cases (50.7%)
> - Lung metastases: 4,167 cases (23.9%)
> - Liver metastases: 3,434 cases (19.7%)
> - Brain metastases: 1,000 cases (5.7%)"
