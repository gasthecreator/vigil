# Excerpt: Imaging Accuracy for Bone Metastases, All Cancers (Yang et al. 2011)

**Source:** Yang HL, Liu T, Wang XM, Xu Y, Deng SM. "Diagnosis of bone metastases: a meta-analysis comparing 18FDG PET, CT, MRI and bone scintigraphy." *Eur Radiol*. 2011;21(12):2604-2617.  
**DOI:** 10.1007/s00330-011-2221-4  
**PMID:** 21887484  
**License:** Springer-Verlag 2011. Only the PubMed abstract is quoted.  

---

## Verbatim Excerpt: Abstract, Results (per-patient basis)

> "On per-patient basis, the pooled sensitivity estimates for PET, CT, MRI and BS were 89.7%, 72.9%, 90.6% and 86.0% respectively."

> "The pooled specificity estimates for PET, CT, MRI and BS were 96.8%, 94.8%, 95.4% and 81.4% respectively."

---

## Limits (not quoted)
- All cancers, not breast cancer specifically.
- Pooled point estimates only; the abstract gives no confidence intervals.
- Literature searched January 1995 to January 2010.
- The per-lesion estimates in the same abstract are not used by M4.
