# Excerpt: Imaging Accuracy for Bone Metastases in Breast Cancer (Liu et al. 2011)

**Source:** Liu T, Cheng T, Xu W, Yan WL, Liu J, Yang HL. "A meta-analysis of 18FDG-PET, MRI and bone scintigraphy for diagnosis of bone metastases in patients with breast cancer." *Skeletal Radiol*. 2011;40(5):523-531.  
**DOI:** 10.1007/s00256-010-0963-8  
**PMID:** 20495798  
**License:** Springer-Verlag 2010. Only the PubMed abstract is quoted.  

---

## Verbatim Excerpt: Abstract, Results (per-patient basis)

> "On a per-patient basis, the pooled sensitivity estimates for MRI (97.1%) were significantly higher than those for PET (83.3%) and BS (87.0%; P <0.05)."

> "The pooled specificity estimates for PET (94.5%) and MRI (97.0%) were both significantly higher than those for BS (88.1%; P <0.05)."

---

## Limits (not quoted)
- Breast cancer population.
- CT was not analysed: the abstract says the number of published CT studies was inadequate for meta-analysis.
- Pooled point estimates only; no confidence intervals in the abstract.
- Same author group and overlapping search window (to January 2010) as E21a, so the two are not independent.
