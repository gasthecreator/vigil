## Verbatim Excerpt: Abstract (Findings)

From Abstract ("Findings"):
> "83 studies were included, which consisted of 26 788 women from 17 sub-Saharan African countries. There was wide between-study heterogeneity in the percentage of late-stage disease at diagnosis (median 74·7%, range 30·3-100%, I2=93·3%, p<0·0001)."
