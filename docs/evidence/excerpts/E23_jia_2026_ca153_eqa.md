## Limits (not quoted)
- Laboratories participating in a Chinese national programme; not Nigerian laboratories, and not the facilities Vigil would serve.
- A robust coefficient of variation across laboratories for the same distributed material, within reagent classes: it is a spread of results between laboratories, which mixes inter-laboratory bias with imprecision, and it is reported as a percentage, not as a log-scale bias.
- The abstract also says that different reagents and methods differ, and that different reference intervals and cut-offs should be used; the study of K5 (`experiments/EXP-K5-001/PREREG.md`) uses the 6.48 to 7.63 percent range only to choose a plausible simulated spread, and labels the larger spreads as assumptions.
