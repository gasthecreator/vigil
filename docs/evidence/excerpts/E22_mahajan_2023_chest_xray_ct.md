## Figures read from the full text (NOT machine-verified by `scripts/audit_excerpts.py`)
These were read on 2026-10-18 from the PMC page for PMC10766925 and are kept outside any quotation because the audit script cannot confirm them.
- Results section: chest radiograph sensitivity 49 percent and specificity 88 percent (the abstract, Discussion and conclusion say 89 percent; the paper is inconsistent with itself by one point).
- Discussion: the authors state that reported sensitivity and specificity of chest radiographs in detecting lung metastases range between 20 and 50 percent and between 90 and 98 percent respectively (their references 23 and 24, not retrieved by M4).

## Limits (not quoted)
- Head and neck squamous cell cancer, not breast cancer.
- One centre (Mumbai), retrospective.
- The chest CT is the reference standard for the radiograph comparison, so the CT figures are the CT against its own scoring and should not be read as a CT accuracy against pathology.
- No confidence intervals.
- A breast-cancer-specific accuracy source for chest radiography was searched for and not found.
