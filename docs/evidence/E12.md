## 2. Verbatim Excerpts & Exact Reported Numbers

### From Wu et al. 2017 (*Oncotarget*, 8:27990–27996):
> **Abstract / Results:**
> "Using the 2010–2013 Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results Program (SEER) data, a retrospective, population-based cohort study to investigate tumor subtypes-specific differences in the sites of distant metastasis. Metastatic patterns information was provided for bone, brain, liver and lung."
> 
> "A total of **243,896 patients**, including 226,451 cases in control groups were identified."
> 
> "Among patients with distant metastasis:
> - **Bone metastasis** was found in **8,848 cases** (predominant across all subtypes; up to 5.1% overall in HR+/HER2+).
> - **Lung metastasis** was found in **4,167 cases**.
> - **Liver metastasis** was found in **3,434 cases** (more frequently observed in HER2-positive subtypes compared with HER2-negative subtypes).
> - **Brain metastasis** was found in **1,000 cases** (HR-/HER2+ subtype patients had higher probability of brain metastasis [OR = 1.978] compared to HR+/HER2-)."
> 
> "Conclusions: The pathological subtypes of breast cancer are clearly different in metastatic behavior with regard to the sites of distant metastasis, emphasizing that this knowledge may help to determine the appropriate strategy for follow-up and guide personalized medicine."

## 3. How These Figures Are Used in Vigil

1. **Baseline Organ Susceptibility Model (Model M1):**
   - Direct empirical prior weights for organ tropism conditioned on primary tumor molecular subtype (HR+/HER2-, HR+/HER2+, HR-/HER2+, Triple Negative).
   - Bone represents the most frequent site of distant metastasis overall (50.7% of all documented site events in Wu 2017), followed by lung (23.9%), liver (19.7%), and brain (5.7%).
   - Visceral tropism (liver/brain) is markedly elevated in HER2-positive and Triple-Negative subtypes relative to luminal HR+/HER2- disease.
2. **Latent Compartment Seeding (`vigil-sim`):**
   - Calibrates the multi-compartment metastatic seeding probabilities across `b_bone`, `b_liver`, `b_lung`, and `b_brain` in generators G1, G2, and G3.
