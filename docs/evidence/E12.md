**Status:** `SECONDARY`

## Verification status

The article identity is established, but this dossier does not currently establish the exact denominators and subtype-specific estimates needed to support model priors. The former dossier's derived site percentages and its use of the total study cohort as a confidence basis are not sufficient evidence for conditional organ probabilities. The saved excerpt is historical material and must not be treated as validating the former M1 implementation.

The Evidence Register classifies E12 as `SECONDARY`. That status controls until the full source tables, study population, overlapping-site counts, subtype definitions, denominator, and target estimand are reconciled and independently checked. No numeric subtype prior is endorsed by this dossier.

## Product use

E12 currently supports no M1 inference or M4 prior. The legacy M1 implementation and package conversion path are quarantined under [ADR-0051](../adr/0051-quarantine-unsupported-m1-priors.md); historical outputs must not be used for patient inference, model selection, or calibration. The API quarantine is recorded in ADR-0041.
