## 2. Verbatim Excerpts & Exact Reported Numbers

### From Molina et al. 1995 (*Breast Cancer Res Treat*, 36:41–48):
> **Abstract / Text:**
> "To evaluate the utility of CEA and CA 15.3 for early diagnosis of recurrence, serial serum determinations of both antigens were performed in **1023 patients** (follow-up: 1–10 years, mean 6.2 years) with primary breast cancer (CA 15.3 in 533 cases) and no evidence of residual disease (NED) after radical treatment... **246 patients** developed metastases during follow-up."
> 
> "CEA and CA 15.3 were elevated (> 10 ng/ml or > 60 U/ml, respectively) prior to diagnosis in 40% (98/246) and **41% (37/91)** of the patients with recurrence, with a lead time of 4.9 +/- 2.2 and **4.2 +/- 2.3 months**, respectively."
> 
> "When patients with locoregional recurrences were excluded, sensitivity improved to 46% (CEA) and **54% (CA 15.3)**, and to **64% with both tumor markers** (CEA and/or CA 15.3)."
> 
> "Specificity of the tumor markers was **99%** for both CEA (777 NED patients) and for CA 15.3 (444 NED patients), respectively."

### From Duffy 2006 (*Clin Chem*, 52:345–351):
> **Abstract / Text:**
> "Although serial determinations of tumor markers after primary treatment for breast cancer can preclinically detect recurrent/metastatic disease with lead times of approximately **2–9 months**, the clinical value of this lead time remains to be determined."
> 
> "At present, the most important clinical application of CA 15-3 is in monitoring therapy in patients with advanced breast cancer that is not assessable by existing clinical or radiologic procedures."

### From Khatcheressian et al. 2006 (*J Clin Oncol*, 24:5091–5097):
> **Abstract (Recommendations):**
> "The use of CBCs, chemistry panels, bone scans, chest radiographs, liver ultrasounds, computed tomography scans, [18F]fluorodeoxyglucose-positron emission tomography scanning, magnetic resonance imaging, or tumor markers (carcinoembryonic antigen, CA 15-3, and CA 27.29) is not recommended for routine breast cancer follow-up in an otherwise asymptomatic patient with no specific findings on clinical examination."

*(Guideline Full-Text):* "Present data are insufficient to recommend the routine use of CA 15-3 or CEA for monitoring patients with breast cancer for disease recurrence."

## 3. How These Figures Are Used in Vigil

1. **Simulator Calibration Targets (`packages/vigil-sim`):**
   - The simulator G1/G2/G3 cohorts are **tuned to reproduce** the Molina 1995 empirical parameters:
     - Mean lead time: $4.2 \pm 2.3$ months ($126 \pm 69$ days).
     - Pre-recurrence sensitivity: $54\%$ (CA 15-3 alone) and $64\%$ (CA 15-3 + CEA) in distant metastatic recurrences.
     - Specificity: $\ge 98\%$ on recurrence-free surveillance trajectories.
   - We explicitly state in code and documentation: *"The generator is tuned to reproduce reported figures from Molina et al. 1995 (n=1,023)."* We do not claim this as an independent finding.
2. **Guideline Positioning:**
   - As emphasized by Khatcheressian 2006 and Duffy 2006, static unselected marker screening is discouraged by ASCO because trials in the 1990s showed no overall survival advantage.
   - Vigil does **not** position serial markers as a screening replacement for imaging, but as an individualized longitudinal anomaly detection and triage engine to prioritize diagnostic workups in resource-constrained environments where routine PET/CT scans are not available.
