# Vigil — Clinical & Research Disclaimer

> **MANDATORY NOTICE**: This disclaimer must accompany every deployment, user interface, export, API response, and document produced by or relating to Vigil.

**Vigil is a research prototype and decision-support aid. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and must not replace the judgement of a qualified clinician. Outputs may be wrong. If you are worried about your health, contact your doctor or hospital.**

---

### Emergency Notice for Patients & Families
If you or your family member experience severe pain, difficulty breathing, acute confusion, sudden swelling, or unexplained bleeding, please proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department or call emergency services. Do not wait for an app notification or scheduled clinic appointment.

### Research and Synthetic Data Notice
Until formal prospective and retrospective clinical validation (Maturity Levels L3–L5 in `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` §18) is complete, all quantitative risk scores, trajectories, and simulations are generated on synthetic and research benchmark data. They must be interpreted solely as demonstration of methodology, not as patient-specific prognostic indicators.
