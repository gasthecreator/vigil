# K1 research module status

**Identifier:** `MODEL-K1-KINETIC-HYBRID`

**Status:** Research-only parameter-inference code; not available for recurrence-risk prediction or patient use.

**Canonical API:** `vigil_ml.k1`; legacy `vigil_ml.kinetic_k1.HybridKineticModelK1` resolves to the same class.

**Current repair study:** [EXP-K1-004 preregistration](../../experiments/EXP-K1-004/PREREG.md).

**Audit:** [Core ML audit](../audits/CORE-ML-AUDIT-2026-10.md).

**Event definition proposal:** [PROP-K1-EVENT-SEMANTICS](../proposals/PROP-K1-EVENT-SEMANTICS.md).

## Supported behavior

K1 currently represents a four-parameter, single-analyte synthetic model: tumor doubling time, CA 15-3 shedding, marker clearance, and a seed day. Numeric assumptions are centralized in [`k1_assumptions.json`](../../packages/vigil-ml/vigil_ml/models/k1_assumptions.json). That file labels them as synthetic research assumptions and lists omitted treatment, laboratory, missingness, censoring, competing-event and verification processes. It does not claim clinical calibration.

The module exposes amortized recognition, a sequential particle filter (under the contract compatibility key `ukf`), NumPyro NUTS, and a conditional Gaussian-mixture NPE route. Neural estimators require committed safetensors generated by the preregistered training script. No route trains during import or patient inference. Route validity, uncertainty calibration, recovery and transfer remain unestablished until EXP-K1-004 outputs and the immutable acceptance suite have been reviewed.

`K1Observations` rejects boolean time and measurement values. The simulator also validates schedules, parameters and noise overrides before numeric conversion. Boolean masks must remain separate from numeric analyte values; coercing `True`/`False` into `1.0`/`0.0` recreates a documented failure in the retracted K1 studies.

The only event quantity available from these four parameters is the posterior probability that the modeled burden doubles once by an absolute study day. It is a synthetic model event, not the `vigil-sim` recurrence estimand. `HybridKineticModelK1.predict_risk` therefore fails closed. The existing recurrence-risk gate is under overseer review; see the event-semantics proposal. No recurrence, diagnosis, reassurance or clinical-use claim is supported.

## Retracted historical evidence

The previous K1 card and EXP-K1-001 through EXP-K1-003 reports described invalid recurrence-risk behavior. Their experiment inputs used boolean measurement masks as CA 15-3 values, and their post-hoc interventions did not implement the registered model changes. Those outputs and the former nine-state label-trained implementation are retracted for model selection and recurrence-risk use. Historical reports remain in the repository as records and must not be read as current evidence.

## Acceptance and evidence boundary

The overseer-owned contract is documented in [`K1-CONTRACT.md`](../ml/K1-CONTRACT.md). Acceptance tests are unchanged. A contract pass would establish only the behaviors tested in its synthetic worlds; it would not establish clinical validity. K1 remains research-only until event semantics, simulator alignment, posterior diagnostics, calibration, robustness, runtime, safety and independent review have evidence.
