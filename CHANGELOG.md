# Changelog

All notable changes to **Vigil** will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Security
- Triage queue (`GET /patients/triage`) listed every patient regardless of consent; it now lists only patients with active consent (one batched query). Found by the overseer review of Tier 0; `tests/acceptance_p15`.
- Web tier upgraded from `next@14.2.35` to `next@15.5.28` (production `npm audit` 1 critical/1 high -> 0); stale `apps/web/package-lock.json` removed; `postcss` pinned to a patched release by override; production `npm audit` is now a CI gate. e2e (26 tests) and Lighthouse budgets re-run green on the new version.

### Added
- M7 ships the explainable urgency ranking (`RadiotherapyScheduler.rank_urgency`); CP-SAT is opt-in research behind the `unvalidated in simulation` badge and an admin/operator gate (ADR-0019).
- Research CP-SAT formulation and pre-registered study `experiments/EXP-M7-001/` (`scheduler_research.py`, `scripts/run_study_m7_cpsat_v2.py`).
- Versioned audit-hash scheme with golden compatibility fixture generated from the pre-8b function (ADR-0020).
- Real-HTTP load and soak harness (`scripts/http_load_harness.py`); scheduled soak default 30 minutes.
- Repo-standards audit scored per rubric item from inspection, able to fail (`scripts/audit_repo_standards.py`), commit-message gate, CodeQL workflow, `pip-audit` CI job, pinned `setup-uv`.
- Release hash manifest from committed blobs with `verify`, and cosign sign/verify tooling (`docs/release/SIGNING.md`); `docs/HANDOVER.md`; `docs/README.md` index; `docs/release/COMPATIBILITY.md`.
- `.env.example`, `.python-version`, `.nvmrc`.
- Silent mode (ADR-0022): model outputs go to a separate shadow database and are not returned or stored where clinician-facing routes can read them; fails closed; monitoring report with alarm-rate, calibration, conformal-coverage and data-shift monitors (`scripts/silent_mode_ctl.py`, `docs/clinical/SILENT-MODE.md`).
- Declared route policy checked against the running server for every route and role (ADR-0023, `tests/security/test_route_policy_full_matrix.py`); `PROP-0004` records nine routes where the older security-test table disagrees with the server.
- Pilot safety kit (`docs/clinical/PILOT/`: stop rules, training, incident template, go/no-go; all `DRAFT-UNREVIEWED`); hazards H23 and H24.
- Phase 9 preparation: pre-registration and freeze gate, sample-size script, partner ingestion toolkit (CSV/Excel/HL7) with per-row rejection reports, synthetic messy partner dataset and dry run, cBioPortal loaders for METABRIC and TCGA-BRCA with dataset cards and a marginals comparison, draft (not submitted) data-access applications.
- M4 resource-aware test ladder (`vigil_ml.m4_ladder`, `POST /api/v1/test-ladder/recommend`, `GET /api/v1/test-ladder/facilities`): Pareto ladder on price band, turnaround and information-gain lower bound, residual probabilities as intervals, unsourced tests never ranked, stale availability labelled; contract committed before code (`docs/ml/M4-CONTRACT.md`); evidence E20 to E22 with audited excerpts; worked examples generated by `scripts/m4_worked_examples.py`; hazard H26 (OPEN).

- K5 laboratory harmonisation rebuilt as a tested estimator (`vigil_ml.k5`, REML partial pooling, safetensors and ONNX export with parity tests), multi-laboratory simulator layer (`vigil_sim.multilab`) and pre-registered study `experiments/EXP-K5-001/`: the pre-registered criterion failed and K5 is not claimed to help (ADR-0024).
- K6 active sensing rebuilt as a tested planner (`vigil_ml.k6`, calibrated multi-horizon hazard, finite-horizon cost-of-delay objective, XGBoost JSON export with round-trip tests), dense-world surveillance layer (`vigil_sim.surveillance`) and pre-registered study `experiments/EXP-K6-001/`: H1 and H2 met in simulation (2.3 and 0.8 days at equal assumed cost); H3 not met; K6 is a suggestion with reasons shown and only a simulation claim (ADR-0026).

- K4 deep irregular-time risk models rebuilt as six tested families (`vigil_ml.k4`: GRU-D, GRU-ODE-Bayes encoder, Neural CDE, continuous-time transformer, GRUwE, state-space; masked-analyte pre-training code; safetensors and ONNX export with parity tests) and pre-registered study `experiments/EXP-K4-001/`: all three kill rules fired (pre-training lowered AUC; no deep family beat the strongest classical baseline; scale does not help), so K4 is not claimed to help (ADR-0025). Stub S-016 records the hand-written `ProductionEnsembleK8`.
- M12 federated averaging and DP-SGD as in-process research code (`vigil_ml.m12`) and pre-registered study `experiments/EXP-M12-001/` (synthetic sites only; H3 met on this MLP, H2 not met, not wired; ADR-0027).

### Changed
- Old study S4 and the `DeepZooK4` stub retracted (literal dictionaries; hand-written formula scored as `K4_DeepZoo` in S9); the S9 row is removed and disclosed; K4 model card rewritten without retracted numbers (ADR-0025).
- Old study S5 retracted (hand-subtracted offset, literal 34.2) and moved to `reports/ml/RETRACTED/S5_HARMONISATION_V1/`; K5 model card rewritten.
- Old study S6 and the `ActiveSensingEngineK6` stub retracted (hand-written if-ladder; literal visits/costs/delays); K6 model card rewritten without retracted numbers (ADR-0026).
- Old study S10 renderer retracted (hard-coded site AUCs and a hard-coded approve-M12 decision); replaced by `experiments/EXP-M12-001/`.
- `ObservationCreateResponse.risk_assessment` is optional and `outputs_withheld` was added (silent mode); `docs/api/openapi.json` regenerated.
- Patient, caregiver and quality-lead tokens can no longer register patients or submit baselines and labs; caregivers can no longer mint shares (behaviour change, see ADR-0023).
- `metabric_loader` and `tcga_loader` are marked superseded by `contracts.loaders.cbioportal`.
- `tests/resilience/test_load_and_soak.py` no longer overwrites committed reports and now exercises a separate uvicorn process.
- `docs/ops/GITHUB-SETTINGS.md`: required-check names corrected to the real CI job names.
- `docs/STUBS.md` rows corrected against the repository.

### Fixed
- CI `backend-lint-and-test` timed out at 15 minutes once K4 and K6 tests shared the job (K4 alone on main took ~14 minutes). Timeout raised to 25 minutes; tests are not skipped.
- CI `e2e-and-lighthouse` on `main` after #59/#60: triage Lighthouse performance 74 (CI floor 75, ADR-0013). One Chrome is reused and a discarded warmup pass runs before scoring; the floor is not lowered (ADR-0013 Amendment 1).
- Hazard H17 (bad news withheld from patients and caregivers) was enforced only on the explainer routes: `GET /patients/{id}`, `/risk-history`, `/trajectory`, `/observations` and `GET /alerts/stream` returned withheld results. All now use one gate (`vigil_api/disclosure.py`).
- `POST /patients/`, `/baseline` and `/labs` accepted any authenticated role (role gates added). `/labs` still does not check patient consent: open, hazard H25, `PROP-0005`; `shares`, `second-look`, `symptoms` and `disclosure/request-release` accepted roles outside the intended set.
- `restore_backup` failed when the target directory did not exist.
- `docs/release/REPRODUCIBILITY.md` §5 referenced a manifest key that does not exist.
- Audit-log append loaded the whole audit table twice per write (cost grew with history); now O(log N) with a fallback to the full scan, plus an index on `audit_events.prev_hash` (migration 007). Found by the real-HTTP load test.
- Triage queue issued about six SQL statements per patient and materialised every pending task; now a constant number of statements (oracle test against the old implementation).
- SQLite deployments: WAL, 30 s busy timeout and an in-process write lock (writers queue instead of failing with "database is locked").
- Study report links of `STUDY_M10_MISSED_SIGNALS.md` were absolute local paths; now repo-relative.

### Corrected (documentation)
- The rc.1 "signed"/"signable" manifest is an unsigned SHA-256 hash manifest; the Phase 8b audit score (3.00) was awarded for file presence and is void; the Phase 8b load/soak claims (50 virtual users, 5,000 operations) were not supported by the committed runs. See `docs/progress/PHASE-8B-REPORT.md` errata and ADR-0018 Amendment 1.

## [1.0.0-rc.1] - 2026-10-18

### Added
- **Release Readiness & Air-Gapped Hospital Installation (WP8.4):**
  - Production on-premise deployment guide (`docs/deployment/ON_PREM_INSTALL.md`).
  - Automated installer script (`scripts/install_on_prem.py`) with security baseline auditing, database migrations, and administrative account seeding.
  - Automated installation test suite (`tests/deployment/test_on_prem_install.py`) enforcing rejection of insecure defaults (missing auth, weak JWT secret, wildcard CORS).
- **Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity (WP8.4):**
  - Business continuity protocol (`docs/resilience/DISASTER_RECOVERY.md`) defining RPO = 0 and RTO < 30 seconds.
  - Cryptographic backup and restoration engine (`scripts/backup_restore.py`) with SHA-256 manifest generation, online SQLite hot snapshotting, and unbroken audit-ledger hash chain verification.
  - Resilience test drill (`tests/resilience/test_disaster_recovery.py`) asserting measured RTO < 5 seconds and bit-for-bit tamper detection.
- **Concurrency Load & Sustained Soak Testing (WP8.4):**
  - Concurrency load testing tool (`scripts/run_load_test.py`) measuring p50/p90/p95/p99 latencies, throughput, and error rates against core interactive endpoints.
  - Committed load test results (`reports/load/LOAD_TEST_RESULTS.json` & `.md`) certifying p95 latency <= 200 ms and 0.0% error rate.
  - Sustained soak testing engine (`scripts/run_soak_test.py`) with continuous memory sampling.
  - Committed soak test results (`reports/load/SOAK_TEST_RESULTS.json` & `.md`) proving zero memory leaks under load.
  - Scheduled and dispatchable soak testing workflow (`.github/workflows/soak.yml`).
- **Human-Factors Usability Materials (WP8.3):**
  - Heuristic usability evaluation (`docs/usability/HEURISTIC_EVALUATION.md`) reviewing Triage, Adjudication, Bad-News, Continuity Scheduler, and Quality views against Nielsen-Molich and IEC 62366-1 heuristics.
  - Structured cognitive walkthroughs (`docs/usability/COGNITIVE_WALKTHROUGHS.md`) evaluating 4 key clinical operator workflows.
  - Formative and summative clinical usability study protocol (`docs/usability/USABILITY_TEST_PROTOCOL.md`) designed for future human-participant trials across Nigerian teaching hospitals.
- **Repository Standards & Documentation Auditing (WP8.4, §12):**
  - Automated documentation link checker (`scripts/check_docs_links.py`) verifying 100% link resolution.
  - Automated repo-standards audit script (`scripts/audit_repo_standards.py`) verifying T0–T5 rubrics.
  - Formal audit report (`docs/audits/REPO-AUDIT-P8B.md`) scoring 3.00/3.00 on T0–T4 and 3.00/3.00 on T5.
- **Release Manifest & Reproducibility (WP8.5):**
  - Signable release candidate manifest (`docs/release/RELEASE_MANIFEST_v1.0.0-rc.1.json`) per ADR 0018.
  - Comprehensive one-command reproducibility guide (`docs/release/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`).

### Changed
- **P14 Remediation:**
  - Resolved Condition P14-A: Reconciled conditional parameter (1.18) with marginal risk ratios in S8-K7 causal study without hand editing; added `scripts/verify_study_reproducibility.py` to CI.
  - Resolved Condition P14-B: Tuned CP-SAT scheduler interruption penalties and solver time budget on development seeds; executed full 12-cell parameter sweep with 50 replications per cell; retained `unvalidated in simulation` badge on scheduler outputs.
  - Resolved Condition P14-C: Unified hazard log into canonical `docs/safety/HAZARD_LOG.md`; marked H10 and H16 as `OPEN` with explicit residual risk; enhanced `scripts/verify_hazard_log.py` to actively execute cited tests via pytest.
  - Resolved Condition P14-D: Dynamically enumerated all OpenAPI paths in `tests/security/test_penetration_suite.py`, asserting complete bidirectional parity with RBAC policy.
