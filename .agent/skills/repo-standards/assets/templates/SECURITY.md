# Security Policy

## Supported versions
| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| <latest minor> | yes |
| older | no |

## Reporting a vulnerability
**Do not open a public issue.** Report privately via GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" (Security tab) or email <security-contact@example.com>.

Include: affected version/commit, impact, reproduction steps or proof of concept, and any suggested fix.

## What to expect
- Acknowledgement within **3 business days** (initial response always within 14 days).
- Triage and severity assessment within **7 days**; we will keep you updated.
- Coordinated disclosure: we aim to ship a fix within **90 days** and publish an advisory crediting reporters who wish it. We will request a CVE where appropriate.

## Scope
In scope: <this repository's code, released artifacts, deployment configs>.
Out of scope: <third-party services, social engineering, DoS via volumetric traffic>.

## Safe harbor
Good-faith research following this policy will not be pursued legally.
